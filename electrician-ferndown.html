Local Electricians Covering Ferndown

Ferndown sits on the edge of East Dorset close to the New Forest, and it's grown steadily as a family-friendly commuter town — most of the housing across Ferndown itself, West Moors and Longham is post-war and later, generally in good electrical order but increasingly due for consumer unit upgrades as boards from the 1980s and 90s reach the end of their practical life.

Every job is fully insured and tested to BS 7671, and we're NICEIC registered — meaning we self-certify notifiable work ourselves rather than sending you to deal with building control separately.

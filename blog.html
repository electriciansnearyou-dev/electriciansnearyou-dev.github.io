Common Questions

The questions we get asked most, answered straight.

What is an EICR?

An Electrical Installation Condition Report is a safety check that tests the fixed wiring in a property — the consumer unit, sockets, switches and cabling — against current wiring regulations (BS 7671).

How often is an EICR needed?

For rented and commercial properties, at least every 5 years, or sooner if a previous report specifies. Landlords also need a valid report before a new tenancy starts.

What is an Amendment 3 board?

A consumer unit built with a non-combustible metal enclosure, required under Amendment 3 of the wiring regulations to help contain any internal fire risk.

Do you cover emergencies?

Yes, we offer a 24/7 emergency callout service across Poole, Bournemouth and Christchurch for faults, power outages and urgent safety issues.

What causes circuits to trip?

Usually an overloaded circuit, a faulty appliance causing a short, or a genuine earth fault. Occasional trips can be normal; frequent ones are worth having checked.

Are your quotes fully binding?

Our online calculators give a budget estimate based on typical jobs. The final price is confirmed once we've seen the property.

What is the cost of an electrician per hour?

UK rates typically run £45–£80 per hour depending on region, though most small jobs are priced as a fixed fee rather than hourly.

How long does a house rewiring take?

A full rewire on an average home usually takes 4–6 days, depending on size, layout and whether the property is occupied during the work.

What is an emergency call-out fee?

A fixed attendance fee covers getting an engineer to you quickly and making things safe. Any further repair work is quoted on site.

How long does an EICR certificate take?

The inspection itself usually takes 2–4 hours; the written certificate follows within 24–48 hours.

Do downlights need to be fire rated?

Yes — recessed downlights cut into a ceiling need to be fire-rated to maintain the fire resistance of the floor or ceiling above.

Are you Part P registered?

Yes, we're NICEIC registered, which lets us self-certify notifiable work under Part P without you needing to involve building control separately.

When should I replace my fuseboard?

If it still uses rewireable fuses, has no RCD protection, or is made from wood or plastic rather than metal, it's overdue an upgrade.

What is an RCBO and do I need one?

An RCBO protects a single circuit individually, so a fault in one room doesn't cut power to the rest of the house. Modern consumer units use one per circuit.

Can I install my own home EV charger?

No — EV charger installation must be carried out by a qualified electrician under BS 7671 (Section 722), which covers the specific safety requirements for charging equipment.

Do EV charging ports qualify for UK grants?

Yes. As of April 2026, the OZEV grant covers 75% of installation cost up to £500, for on-street parking, renters and flat owners, and landlords — open until 31 March 2027.

What does an EICR "Code C1" mean?

A C1 means danger is present right now. We make it safe immediately, on the spot, before we leave.

What is the difference between C2 and C3 codes?

C2 means potentially dangerous and needs fixing within 28 days — it's what makes a report "Unsatisfactory." C3 is just a recommended improvement, not a fail.

Why do my lightbulbs keep blowing?

Often a loose connection, a fitting rated for a lower wattage than the bulb, or occasionally a voltage issue on the supply. Worth having checked if it keeps happening.

What is an earth loop impedance check?

It measures how quickly a fault current would travel back through the earthing system, confirming your protective devices will trip fast enough to keep you safe.

Are landlord electrical checks tax deductible?

Generally yes — EICR testing and related repair costs on a rental property are usually treated as an allowable expense, but check with your accountant for your specific situation.

What is a Surge Protection Device (SPD)?

It protects your electronics from voltage spikes, caused by things like lightning strikes or grid switching, by safely diverting the excess current to earth.

Can I replace a plug socket faceplate myself?

It's not something we'd recommend without electrical knowledge — the circuit needs to be safely isolated first. A like-for-like swap by a professional is quick and inexpensive.

What is the standard lifespan of home wiring?

Modern PVC-insulated cable typically lasts 30–40 years. Older wiring insulated in rubber or fabric should be assessed for replacement regardless of its exact age.

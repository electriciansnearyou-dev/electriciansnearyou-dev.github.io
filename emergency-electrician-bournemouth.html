24 Hour Emergency Electricians in Bournemouth

Electrical faults don't wait for convenient hours. If you've lost power across the house, smell burning near a socket, or have an RCD that keeps tripping no matter what you switch off, cut the power at the consumer unit if it's safe to do so and call us.

We cover urgent callouts across Bournemouth and the surrounding area — power outages, faulty consumer units, exposed or damaged cabling, and anything that needs making safe immediately. All work is carried out to BS 7671, whatever time we're called out.

Common Emergency Callouts in Bournemouth

🔌

Power Loss

Whether it's the whole property or just part of it, we trace the fault, isolate the damaged section, and safely restore power.

⚡

Tripping RCDs

A circuit that keeps tripping usually means a genuine fault — a damaged cable, a faulty appliance, or water ingress. We test each circuit to find which one and why.

🔥

Burning Smells

A burning or acrid smell near a socket or the consumer unit is a warning sign of a loose or overheating connection. We isolate the area and make it safe immediately.

💧

Water-Damaged Fittings

A leak or flooding near sockets, switches or the consumer unit is dangerous — don't touch anything wet. We isolate the circuit and assess what needs replacing.

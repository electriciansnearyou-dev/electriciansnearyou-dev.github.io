Emergency Electrical Repairs

Electrical emergencies don't announce themselves in advance. A total loss of power, a burning smell near the consumer unit, circuit breakers that keep tripping, or exposed live wiring — any of these need immediate attention, not a diary appointment.

We provide rapid diagnostic fault-finding and emergency repairs across Dorset and the Hampshire border for homeowners, landlords, and businesses. Every callout is handled by a qualified engineer and carried out to BS 7671, whatever time we're called.

Common Electrical Emergencies We Resolve

🔌

Power Outages

Whether it's the whole property or just part of it, we trace the cause and safely restore power.

⚡

Tripping Electrics

Repeated trips usually mean a genuine fault. We test each circuit to find which one, and why, rather than just resetting it.

🔥

Burning Smells

An acrid smell near a socket or the consumer unit means a loose or overheating connection. We isolate it and make it safe immediately.

🛡

Faulty Fuse Boards

From a single failed breaker to a board that's given up entirely, we repair or replace what's needed to get you safely back up and running.

⚠️

Exposed Wiring

If cabling's been damaged or exposed during other work, we isolate it, make it safe, and reinstate proper protection.

🔍

Advanced Fault Finding

Intermittent or hidden faults inside walls or behind fittings — we use proper test equipment to track them down rather than guessing.

Emergency Electricians

Fast response fault correction across Dorset.

EV Charger Installation

Home charging points, fully certified.

Professional installation of home EV charging systems with safe, compliant wiring and testing. We currently install high-quality Ohme and BG chargers.
🏠

House Rewiring

Full or partial rewires for any property.

Complete or partial rewiring services to improve safety, efficiency and compliance with the latest regulations. Contact us to check if your system truly needs a partial or full upgrade.

Fuse Board Upgrades

Modern units with surge protection.

Replacement of old boards with Amendment 3 metal consumer units packed with individual circuit RCBO switches and built-in surge protective devices.

Lighting Installation

Indoor, outdoor, LED and security beams.

Installation and replacement of ceiling lights, spotlights, LED upgrades, garden configurations, and external security lights tailored around your premises.
🔌

Accessory Replacements

Sockets, switches, isolators, cooker points.

Replacing old or broken fittings safely. We also offer Air Flow testing to improve air quality and help keep damp and humidity out of your property.
